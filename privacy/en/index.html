Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Who Is Responsible for Data Processing

Data controller: Individual Entrepreneur Ivan Sergeevich Abbazyan (Аббазян Иван Сергеевич), operating under the DEMIXRA gaming brand. Contact email: demixra@list.ru.

2. Applications Covered by This Policy

This policy applies to DEMIXRA mobile games and applications that link to it. It also applies to future games, provided that this document accurately describes their data processing practices.

3. Saving Game Progress

Games may store progress, scores, settings, and achievements on the user’s device. Where no cloud features are provided, this data remains stored locally.

4. Advertising

Free games may use the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on the implementation and permissions, the advertising provider may process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, ad impression and interaction data, and diagnostic data.

Information about the advertising platform and its documentation: Yandex Mobile Ads.

5. Purposes of Processing and Recipients

Game data is used to operate the application and save progress. Advertising SDK data may be processed to display advertisements, measure effectiveness, maintain security, and prevent fraud. Recipients of advertising data may include Yandex and other parties involved in processing through its services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

6.1. Local Game Data

Game progress, earned points, settings, and other local data are stored on the user’s device while the relevant application is in use.

The developer does not set a fixed automatic deletion period for this data.

Users can delete local game data using operating system tools or in-app progress-reset features, where available.

6.2. Advertising Service Data

When Yandex Mobile Ads is used, certain technical data may be processed and stored by the advertising partner.

Retention periods for such data are determined by the advertising partner’s applicable policies, the purposes of processing, and legal requirements.

DEMIXRA does not independently set retention periods for data stored in the advertising partner’s systems.

6.3. User Requests

For questions regarding the processing or deletion of personal data, users can contact: demixra@list.ru.

Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law. If data is processed by the advertising partner, users may also need to contact the relevant data controller directly.

7. International Processing and Data Security

DEMIXRA mobile games and applications may be used by users in different countries.

Applications may use the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising. During its operation, certain technical data and device identifiers may be transferred to the advertising partner.

Depending on the advertising partner’s infrastructure, data may be processed outside the user’s country of residence. Such processing must comply with applicable data protection law.

The Yandex Mobile Ads SDK uses HTTPS/TLS encryption to protect data in transit.

DEMIXRA takes steps to comply with applicable personal data protection requirements, including rules on international data transfers and obtaining user consent where required.

8. Children

DEMIXRA mobile games may have different age ratings depending on each application’s content.

Each game’s age rating is indicated on its listing in the relevant app store.

When using advertising technology, DEMIXRA takes into account child protection laws and the advertising policies of the relevant platforms.

Applications intended for children may be subject to additional restrictions on personal data collection, personalized advertising, and use of advertising identifiers.

Advertising shown to minors must comply with applicable age restrictions and legal requirements.

If the required level of protection for a child audience cannot be provided, advertisements will not be shown to that audience.
